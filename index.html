How to read this

The dot shows the shape of your vowel. Up and down follows the first resonance of your vocal tract, which rises as you open. Left and right follows the second, which rises as the vowel gets brighter and more forward. Slide slowly from oo through oh, ah, eh to ee and watch it travel.

Sing an ah you like and tap Set my ah. The pad then zooms in on that spot and the circle marks it. Stay inside the circle while you change pitch, dynamics or breath.

Ring compares the energy around 2 to 4 kHz with everything below. It moves with mouth distance, so keep the microphone in one place.

Above roughly the F4 above middle C the dot gets less trustworthy, because the harmonics become too sparse to outline the resonances.
